Company Setup & Master Data

This section outlines the basic structural configuration of the organization. The master data forms the organizational skeletal framework that scopes security filters, defines reporting hierarchies, and establishes boundaries for career movements.

Hierarchical Cascading Structure Diagram

                                graph TD
                                    Group[Group Level 0] -->|Contains| Company[Company Level 1]
                                    CompanyType[Company Type] -->|Classifies| Company
                                    Company -->|Has| Location[Location / Business Unit Level 2]
                                    Location -->|Has| Division[Division Level 3]
                                    Division -->|Has| Department[Department Level 4]
                                    Department -->|Has| Section[Section Level 5]
                                    Section -->|Applies to| Employee[Employee Placement]

1. Groups Setup

Level 0

Purpose: The Groups model represents the parent holding company or parent conglomerate (e.g. Innovity Technologies Inc). It acts as the root node of the system, under which multiple subsidiaries are created.

Business Logic & Rules: Deleting a Group runs cascading validation checks to prevent orphaned Companies or Location entities. All users and access credentials are filtered using this parent structure if multi-group controls are active.

How to Do the Task: Go to Company Setup > Groups, click Create, fill out the name, toggle the status to Active, and click Save.

2. Company Types

Metadata Classification

Purpose: Used to define regulatory classifications of entities (e.g. Pvt Ltd, Joint Venture, NGO, Partnership). Classification assists in compliance reporting and tax groupings.

Business Logic & Rules: A Company Type cannot be deleted if there is at least one active company referencing it. Mapped to the `Company` entity via foreign key validation rules.

How to Do the Task: Go to Company Setup > Company Types, click Create, input the name, select Active, and click Save.

3. Companies Setup

Level 1

Purpose: Establishes commercial subsidiaries under the corporate parent group. Each company acts as an isolated entity with its own tax registry number, logo branding, and payroll structure.

Business Logic & Rules: Must map to valid `groups.id` and `company_types.id` keys. Mapped as the primary contextual filter for security constraints; query scoping policies prevent an HR manager at "Company A" from viewing metadata records belonging to "Company B".

How to Do the Task: Go to Company Setup > Companies, click Create, select the parent Group and Company Type, input the legal name, tax registry code (TIN/BIN), and address, then click Save.

4. Business Units / Branches

Level 2

Purpose: Configures physical locations, plants, corporate offices, or factories. Enables geofencing/IP checks for attendance logging and localized holiday planners.

Business Logic & Rules: Every branch location is dependent on a parent Company. Deleting a company checks and blocks if active locations/branches reference it.

How to Do the Task: Go to Company Setup > Company Locations, click Create, select the parent Company, enter the branch name, input geographic details, and save.

5. Divisions

Level 3

Purpose: Sets broad operational clusters (e.g. Engineering, Sales, Operations). Operational divisions aggregate multiple localized departments.

Business Logic & Rules: In placement and entry forms, selecting a parent Company filters the selectable Divisions to those mapped to that company to ensure structural integrity.

How to Do the Task: Go to Company Setup > Divisions, click Create, select parent Company and Location/Branch, type in the Division name, and save.

6. Departments

Level 4

Purpose: Functional department units (e.g. Software Development, Technical Support, QA). Establishes cost-center boundaries and supervisor/approval channels.

Business Logic & Rules: Requires mapping to a parent `division_id`. Automatically inherits security scoping constraints from the parent company model.

How to Do the Task: Go to Company Setup > Departments, click Create, select parent Company, Location, and Division, input the department name, and save.

7. Sections Setup

Level 5

Purpose: Granular project or team level mapping (e.g. Mobile App Team, QA Automation Team). Isolates workflows and enables precise employee targeting.

Business Logic & Rules: Mapped directly under `department_id`. Placement forms dynamically load this value based on the selection path: Company > Location > Division > Department > Section.

How to Do the Task: Go to Company Setup > Sections, click Create, select parent Company, Location, Division, and Department, type the section name, and save.

8. Designations

Level Weights

Purpose: Defines corporate job titles (e.g. Tech Lead, VP) and positions employees within the organizational hierarchy.

Business Logic & Rules: Every designation contains a numeric `designation_level` (weight). Promotion and demotion logic uses this weight value for validation checks:
Promotion Rule: new_designation_level > current_designation_level (e.g. Level 2 Software Engineer to Level 3 Senior)
Demotion Rule: new_designation_level < current_designation_level (e.g. Level 3 Senior to Level 1 Associate)

How to Do the Task: Go to Company Setup > Designations, click Create, input the title, select/assign the level weight number, choose Active, and click Save.

9. Pay Groups

Payroll Configuration

Purpose: Categorizes employees based on payroll cycles (e.g. Monthly Staff, Weekly Freelancers, Executive Pay). This grouping defines default payment frequencies.

Business Logic & Rules: Enforces processing frequency rules (`Monthly`, `Weekly`, `Bi-Weekly`, `Semi-Monthly`) and maps a payroll processing day (e.g., 25th of the month) to determine cutoff dates for calculations.

How to Do the Task: Go to Company Setup > Pay Groups, click Create, enter the title, select frequency, input processing cutoff day, and save.

10. Salary Grades

Compensation Tier

Purpose: Standardized compensation bands (e.g. Grade 1 (G1), Grade 2 (G2)). Organizes salary progression structures.

Business Logic & Rules: Codes (e.g., `G1`) must be unique. The model includes a `deleting` observer: when a Salary Grade is deleted, all child Pay Scales associated with it are automatically deleted to maintain database integrity.

How to Do the Task: Go to Company Setup > Salary Grades, click Create, input the unique grade code, enter a descriptive title, and save.

11. Pay Scales Setup

Validation Rule Engine

Purpose: Establishes standard compensation limits by mapping a combination of **Salary Grade** and **Pay Group** to a defined minimum and maximum salary range.

Business Logic & Rules: Acts as a validation check when processing salary increments, decrements, promotions, or demotions. If the calculated salary is higher than the max limit or lower than the min limit of the selected scale, the system displays a warning.

How to Do the Task: Go to Company Setup > Pay Scales, click Create, select Salary Grade and Pay Group, enter Minimum and Maximum salary limits, and save.

Salary Bounds Verification Flow Diagram

                                    graph TD
                                        Start[Enter Adjustments in Form] --> Calc[Compute New Gross Salary]
                                        Calc --> Query[Fetch Selected Pay Scale min/max values]
                                        Query --> CheckMax{Is New Gross > Max?}
                                        CheckMax -->|Yes| WarnMax[Show warning: Gross exceeds pay scale max limit]
                                        CheckMax -->|No| CheckMin{Is New Gross < Min?}
                                        CheckMin -->|Yes| WarnMin[Show warning: Gross falls below pay scale min limit]
                                        CheckMin -->|No| Success[Salary is within range - No Warnings]

12. Movement Types

Classification Parameters

Purpose: Classifies employee transitions (e.g. Annual Increment, Correction, Promotion, Performance Demotion, General Transfer). Categorizing adjustments helps identify trends in organizational shifts.

Business Logic & Rules: Mapped directly to adjustment models (Increment, Decrement, Promotion, Demotion, Transfer) using `movement_type_id`. This categorizes adjustments for reporting, historical change tracking, and auditing.

How to Do the Task: Go to Company Setup > Movement Types, click Create, enter the classification name, write an optional description, and save.
